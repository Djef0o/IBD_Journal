IBD Journal is a personal diary for people living with Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis.

• Log stools in two taps: Bristol scale, blood, mucus, pain, fatigue, urgency, false urges
• Evening notification to log stool type, pain and fatigue without opening the app
• Meals with barcode scanning (Open Food Facts)
• Treatments with reminders, adherence and refill alerts
• Colonoscopy and rectosigmoidoscopy: date, diet, preparation doses and a reminder for each step, as prescribed by your doctor
• Lab results: 43 common tests with the reference range printed by your lab, charts and tables; import from your lab's PDF reports, read on the phone
• PDF report for your doctor
• Password-encrypted backup and restore to a file

Privacy: no account, no server, no tracker, no Google services. Your data never leaves the phone unless you export it. The only network use is the barcode lookup on Open Food Facts.

IBD Journal is a personal journal, not a medical device. It does not interpret your results and does not replace medical advice.
