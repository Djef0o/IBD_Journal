IBD Journal is a personal diary for people living with Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis.

• Log stools in two taps: Bristol scale, blood, mucus, pain, fatigue, urgency, false urges
• Evening notification to log stool type, pain and fatigue without opening the app
• Meals with barcode scanning (Open Food Facts)
• Treatments with reminders, adherence and refill alerts
• Colonoscopy and rectosigmoidoscopy: date, diet, preparation doses and a reminder for each step, as prescribed by your doctor
• Daily joint, skin, eye, mouth and fever symptoms, questions for your doctor, appointments with reminders
• Lab results: 47 common tests with the reference range printed by your lab, charts and tables; import from your lab's PDF reports, read on the phone
• Body weight: log your weight with its date and follow your weight curve
• Flares, activity scores (Harvey-Bradshaw, SCCAI), vaccinations with optional reminders and steroid courses
• Meals and symptoms analysis, urgent-need card and emergency sheet readable from the lock screen
• PDF report for your doctor
• Password-encrypted backup and restore to a file, with a monthly reminder
• CSV export (spreadsheet) in a password-protected archive
• Home-screen widgets (add-a-stool button, last 7 days) and an optional weekly weigh-in reminder
• Sleep, stress, mood and period notes, compared with your symptoms in Trends
• Intakes left per treatment, counted down as you confirm doses, with a low-stock notification
• Travel pack: medication to take along, letter for your doctor and urgent-need card in French, English, Spanish or German

Privacy: no account, no server, no tracker, no Google services. Your data never leaves the phone unless you export it. The only network use is the barcode lookup on Open Food Facts.

IBD Journal is a personal journal, not a medical device. It does not interpret your results and does not replace medical advice.
